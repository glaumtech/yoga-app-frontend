## Getting Started

### Accessing the System

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge recommended)
2. Go to the application URL provided by your administrator
3. You'll see the login page | Home page

### Logging In

1. **Enter your credentials**:
   - **Email Address**: Your registered email address
   - **Password**: Your password

2. Click **"Sign In"** button

3. The system will automatically determine your role (Admin, Judge, or Participant) based on your account

**Note:** 
- You don't need to select a role - the system recognizes your role automatically when you log in
- **Sign Up is only for Participants** - If you're a participant and don't have an account, click **"Sign Up"** to create one
- **Admins and Judges** - Your account is created by the administrator. Contact them for your login credentials

## For Administrators

As an administrator, you manage the entire competition system. Here's how to use each feature:

### 1. Creating a New Event

**Before you start:** Make sure you have judges added to the system (see section 2 below).

**Step 1:** After logging in, you'll see the Admin Dashboard

**Step 2:** Click on **"Event Management"** or **"Events"** from the menu

**Step 3:** Click the **"Create"** button (usually a + icon or "Create Event" button)

**Step 4:** Fill in the event details:
- **Event Name**: Give your competition a name (e.g., "State Level Yoga Championship 2025")
- **Description**: Describe the event
- **Start Date**: When the competition begins
- **End Date**: When the competition ends
- **Location**: Where the event will be held
- **Banner Image**: Upload a picture for the event (optional but recommended)

**Step 5:** Click **"Create"** or **"Save"**

✅ **Done!** Your event is now created and visible to participants.

**Next Steps:**
1. Add judges (see section 2)
2. Create teams for the event (see section 5)
3. Wait for participants to register
4. Accept participants (see section 4)
5. Assign participants to teams (see section 6)

---

### 2. Adding a Judge

**Step 1:** Go to **"Judge Management"** from the Admin Dashboard

**Step 2:** Click **"Create"** button

**Step 3:** Fill in the judge information:
- **Name***: Full name of the judge
- **Designation***: Their title (e.g., "Senior Yoga Instructor", "Master Trainer")
- **Username***: A unique username for login (e.g., "judge001")
- **Email***: Valid email address (required)
- **Address**: Physical address
- **Password***: Create a secure password
- **Confirm Password***: Type the password again

**Step 4:** Click **"Create"**

✅ **Done!** The judge can now log in with their username and password.

**Important:** 
- Email is mandatory and must be valid
- Share the username and password with the judge securely
- Judges can change their password after first login

---

### 3. Registering a Participant

**Step 1:** Go to **"Events"** and click on the event you want to register participants for

**Step 2:** In the Event Details page, find the **"Registered Participants"** section

**Step 3:** Click **"Register Participant"** or **"Add Participant"** button

**Step 4:** Fill in the registration form:

**Personal Information:**
- **Name***: Participant's full name
- **Date of Birth***: Select from calendar
- **Gender***: Choose Male or Female
- **Address***: Complete address
- **Photo***: Upload a clear photo of the participant

**Yoga Details:**
- **Yoga Master Name***: Name of their yoga teacher
- **Yoga Master Contact***: Contact number
- **School Name***: Name of their school/institution
- **Group/Standard***: Select appropriate group:
  - II, III
  - IV, V
  - VI, VII
  - VIII, IX
  - X - XII
  - UG / PG

**Event Details:**
- **Categories***: Select one or both:
  - ☑ Common
  - ☑ Special

**Step 5:** Review all information and click **"Submit"** or **"Register"**

✅ **Done!** The participant is now registered for the event.

---

### 4. Accepting Participants

**Step 1:** Go to the Event Details page

**Step 2:** Scroll to **"Registered Participants"** section

**Step 3:** Find the participant you want to accept

**Step 4:** Look for the participant's status (it will show "Pending")

**Step 5:** Click the **"Accept"** or **"Approve"** button next to the participant's name

**Step 6:** Confirm the acceptance

✅ **Done!** The participant's status changes to "Accepted" and they can now be assigned to a team.

**Important:** Participants must be accepted before they can be assigned to teams/judges.

---

### 5. Creating Teams (Jury Groups)

**Step 1:** Go to the **Event Details** page for the event you're managing

**Step 2:** Scroll down to the **"Teams"** section

**Step 3:** Click **"Create Team"** button (usually a + icon or "Create Team" button in the Teams section)

**Step 4:** Fill in team details in the popup form:
- **Team Name***: Enter a name (e.g., "Jury 1", "Jury 2", "Team A", "Team B")
- **Category***: Select a category from the dropdown (Common or Special)
- **Jury (Select Multiple)***: Check the boxes next to the judges you want to add to this team
  - You can select multiple judges (e.g., Jury 1, Jury 2, Jury 3)
  - Each judge will be part of this team

**Step 5:** Click **"Create Team"** button

**Step 6:** The popup will close and you'll see your new team in the Teams section

✅ **Done!** The team is created and ready for participant assignment.

**Tip:** 
- Create multiple teams (Jury 1, Jury 2, etc.) to organize participants into different judging groups
- Each team can have multiple judges assigned to it
- Teams are specific to categories (Common or Special)

---

### 6. Assigning Participants to Teams/Judges

**Step 1:** Go to the Event Details page

**Step 2:** At the top of the page, click the **"Assign Participant"** button (green button next to "View Scores" and "Register Now")

**Step 3:** You'll be taken to the **"Assign Participants"** screen

**Step 4:** Select a **Team** from the dropdown at the top (e.g., "Jury 1", "Jury 2", "Team A")
   - Only teams you've created for this event will appear
   - Make sure you've created teams first (see section 5)

**Step 5:** In the participants list below, you'll see all **Accepted** participants who are not yet assigned
   - Participants show their details: Name, Code, Category, Status
   - Only "Accepted" and "Un Assigned" participants are shown

**Step 6:** Select one or more participants by checking the boxes next to their names
   - You can select multiple participants at once
   - Selected participants will be highlighted

**Step 7:** Click the **"Assign"** button (appears when participants are selected)

**Step 8:** Confirm the assignment

✅ **Done!** The selected participants are now assigned to the team, and all judges in that team will see these participants in their assigned list.

**Important Workflow:**
1. ✅ Participant registers → Status: **Pending**
2. ✅ Admin accepts participant → Status: **Accepted**
3. ✅ Admin creates teams (Jury 1, Jury 2, etc.) with judges
4. ✅ Admin clicks "Assign Participant" button → Selects team → Selects participants → Assigns
5. ✅ All judges in the team can now score the assigned participants

**Tips:**
- You can assign multiple participants to a team at once
- Participants must be "Accepted" before they can be assigned
- Each team can have multiple judges, and all judges in the team will see the assigned participants
- Participants are assigned to teams based on their category (Common or Special)

---

### 5. Viewing Participant Scores

**Step 1:** Go to **"Participant Scores"** from Admin Dashboard

**Step 2:** Select the event from the list

**Step 3:** You'll see all participants with their scores

**Step 4:** Click on any participant's name to see detailed scores:
- Category-wise breakdown (Common/Special)
- Individual asana scores
- Scores from each judge
- Grand total

✅ **Done!** You can now review all scoring details.

---

### 6. Downloading Certificates

**Step 1:** Go to Event Details page

**Step 2:** Find the participant in **"Registered Participants"** section

**Step 3:** Look for the certificate/download icon (📄 or ⬇️) next to the participant

**Step 4:** Click the icon

**Step 5:** The certificate will download as a PDF file

✅ **Done!** The certificate is saved to your downloads folder.

**Note:** Certificates are only available after participants have been scored.

## For Judges

As a judge, you score participants assigned to you. Here's how:

### 1. Logging In

**Step 1:** Go to the login page

**Step 2:** Enter your email address and password (provided by the administrator)

**Step 3:** Click **"Sign In"**

**Step 4:** The system will automatically recognize you as a judge and show your dashboard

---

### 2. Viewing Your Assigned Participants

**Step 1:** After login, navigate to **"Assigned Participants"** (you may need to select an event first)

**Step 2:** You'll see the **"Assigned Participants"** screen with:
   - **Left Side**: List of participants assigned to you
     - Each participant shows as a card/tag with their name
     - Participants are grouped by category (Common/Special)
   - **Right Side**: List of juries/judges in your team
     - Shows which jury/judge group you belong to
   - **Top Right**: A checkmark button (✓) to proceed

**Step 3:** Review the participants and juries shown

**Step 4:** Click the **checkmark button (✓)** in the top right corner to start scoring

**Note:** 
- You can only see participants assigned to your team by the administrator
- Participants must be accepted by admin before they appear in your list
- Participants are assigned to teams (like Jury 1, Jury 2), and you see participants from your team
- The screen shows both participants and juries side by side for reference

---

### 3. Entering Scores

**Step 1:** From the **"Assigned Participants"** screen, click the **checkmark button (✓)** in the top right

**Step 2:** You'll see the **"Add Scores"** screen with:
   - **Participant Information Card** at the top showing:
     - Participant name (e.g., "PARTICIPANT2")
     - Age and gender
     - Standard/Group and School name

**Step 3:** Scroll to the **"Jury Scores"** section

**Step 4:** You'll see **"Tasks (Yoga Poses)"** section with:
   - A default "Task 1" already added
   - Each task has two fields:
     - **Task Name**: Name of the yoga pose/asana (e.g., "Task 1", "Paschimotanasana")
     - **Score**: Enter the score for this task (typically 0-10)

**Step 5:** To add more tasks:
   - Click the **"+ Add Task"** button (green link on the right)
   - A new task row will appear (Task 2, Task 3, etc.)
   - You can add up to 5 tasks maximum
   - Enter the task name and score for each

**Step 6:** Fill in scores for all tasks:
   - Edit task names if needed (e.g., change "Task 1" to actual asana name)
   - Enter scores for each task
   - The **Grand Total** at the bottom will update automatically as you enter scores

**Step 7:** Review all scores and the grand total

**Step 8:** Click the **"Save Scores"** button at the bottom (green button with save icon)

✅ **Done!** Your scores are saved and will be calculated automatically.

**Important:**
- You can add multiple tasks (up to 5) for each participant
- Task names can be customized (e.g., actual asana names)
- Scores are typically on a scale of 0-10
- Grand total is calculated automatically
- Be fair and consistent in your scoring
- You can update scores before final submission
- Multiple judges' scores are averaged for final calculation

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: I forgot my password. What should I do?**
A: Contact your administrator to reset your password.

**Q: Can I use the system on my phone?**
A: Yes! The system works on mobile phones, tablets, and computers.

**Q: Do I need to install anything?**
A: No, just use a web browser. No installation required.

---

### For Administrators

**Q: What is the correct workflow for managing participants?**
A: The workflow is:
1. Participant registers → Status: Pending
2. Admin accepts participant → Status: Accepted
3. Admin creates teams (Jury 1, Jury 2, etc.)
4. Admin assigns accepted participant to a team/judge
5. Judge can score the participant

**Q: Do I need to accept participants before assigning them?**
A: Yes, participants must be accepted first. Only accepted participants can be assigned to teams/judges.

**Q: What are teams and why do I need to create them?**
A: Teams (like Jury 1, Jury 2) help organize participants into different judging groups. Create teams before assigning participants.

**Q: How many judges can I assign to one participant?**
A: You can assign multiple judges. Their scores will be averaged.

**Q: Can I edit an event after creating it?**
A: Yes, click on the event and use the edit option.

**Q: How do I delete a participant?**
A: Contact technical support. Participant deletion is restricted for data integrity.

**Q: Can participants register themselves?**
A: Yes, participants can register themselves. You then need to accept them before assigning to teams.

---

### For Judges

**Q: I don't see any participants assigned to me.**
A: Contact the administrator. They need to assign participants to you first.

**Q: Can I change my scores after submitting?**
A: Yes, you can update scores before the final deadline. Contact admin if you need to change after submission.

**Q: What if I make a mistake in scoring?**
A: You can edit scores before final submission. If already submitted, contact the administrator.

**Q: How are final scores calculated?**
A: Individual asana scores are averaged across all judges, then summed for totals.

---

### For Participants

**Q: How long does registration approval take?**
A: Usually within 24-48 hours. Contact the administrator if it takes longer.

**Q: How do I create an account?**
A: Click "Sign Up" on the login page. Sign up is only available for participants. If you're an admin or judge, contact the administrator for your account.

**Q: Can I register for multiple events?**
A: Yes, you can register for multiple events through the Events page.

**Q: When will I see my scores?**
A: Scores are visible after all assigned judges complete their scoring.

**Q: Can I download my certificate multiple times?**
A: Yes, you can download your certificate as many times as needed.

**Q: What if my photo didn't upload?**
A: Check your internet connection and try again. Make sure the file is JPG or PNG format and not too large.

## Quick Reference Card

### Admin Quick Actions
- **Create Event**: Dashboard → Event Management → Create
- **Add Judge**: Dashboard → Judge Management → Create
- **Create Team**: Event Details → Teams Section → Create Team
- **Register Participant**: Event Details → Register Participant
- **Accept Participant**: Event Details → Participants → Accept (Pending status)
- **Assign to Team**: Event Details → "Assign Participant" button → Select Team → Select Participants → Assign
- **View Scores**: Event Details → "View Scores" button OR Dashboard → Participant Scores

### Admin Workflow Order
1. Create Event
2. Add Judges
3. Create Teams (Jury 1, Jury 2, etc.)
4. Participants Register (or Admin registers them)
5. Accept Participants (change status from Pending to Accepted)
6. Assign Accepted Participants to Teams/Judges
7. Judges Score Participants
8. View Scores and Download Certificates

### Judge Quick Actions
- **View Assignments**: Login → Assigned Participants
- **Enter Scores**: Click Participant → Enter Scores → Submit

### Participant Quick Actions
- **Register**: Events → Select Event → Register
- **View Scores**: My Registrations → Select Event
- **Download Certificate**: Event → Download Icon
